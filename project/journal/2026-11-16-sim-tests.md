# The Wyrm and the sheriff, proved on the cell's server (TC-B5, TC-B6)

> Written by: Claude (opus-brassgun12), 2026-11-16

`test/sim.ts` loads the folder `wildshard check .` builds into the pinned
engine's headless simulation, the way the grid cell server does
(engine `cellWorld.ts`): with the product's sections. That matters: without
them the encounter runs no decision, and the Wyrm falls back to its spawn
row's strike, a burst every 2.5 s and nothing else. A first draft of the
boss test missed that and saw only bursts.

- `test/wyrm.test.ts` (TC-B5): the poster starts the bounty, the player
  stands 5 m off the mound, the first phase lands the burst and the tail
  sweep and never the spit; rounds into the open gullet take it under half
  health, the second phase lands the spit; its death files fact
  `brasscap.wyrm` and pays 20.
- `test/sheriff.test.ts` (TC-B6): offer, accept, camps; "Camp 1 is clear"
  is refused until the three camp-1 bandits are dead; then the gate node;
  "The Dust Wyrm is dead" is refused until `dead:dust-wyrm`; then thanks.

## The whole quest (TC-B8)

`test/quest.test.ts` runs Brasscap Mesa from the sheriff's accept to the
report back, then snapshots the host, restores it into a fresh one and
reads every flag back. It pays 5, 5, 5 (camp bounties), 20 (the Wyrm)
and 25 (the quest).

Found on the way: a player who stands off the cell (400, 400) for a
second while the gate opens leaves the Wyrm `armed` for good; walking
into the arena afterwards never starts its intro. Standing at the depot
instead, it starts. Whether a real walk out of the cell and back does
the same is not checked yet.
